# Brief: Intop Business — whole app in DEMO mode, small/large screens and large text

Read AGENT_BRIEF.md first. Budget: about 90 actions. Screenshot after every action.

The app is started in DEMO mode: a built-in demo business («Демо-кафе» / «Demo cafe»), local made-up data, no account, nothing is sent anywhere. Do NOT sign in, do NOT type any e-mail or password. If a sign-in screen appears, write NOT REACHED for that part and go back.
Inside demo you may tap freely (open lists, cards, sheets, switches of the period). Still do not press «Удалить» / «Delete» / «Выйти» / «Sign out».

## Path
1. First-launch intro (notifications, sound, permissions, summary): go through it with «Не сейчас» / «Not now», «Дальше» / «Next», «Начать работу». If a «Подключите Telegram» sheet appears, press «Пропустить» / «Skip».
2. Home: scroll to the bottom.
3. Every tab of the bottom bar: Orders (open one order), Menu (open one dish), Analytics.
4. «Ещё» / «More»: open EVERY row once (each section), scroll it to the bottom, go back. On a tablet the sections are in the left menu — open each.

## On every screen write down (with screenshot id)
- A word broken with a hyphen at the end of a line (e.g. «устрой-ствах», «пропу-стите», «Пригла-сить»). Give the exact text.
- A money amount split across two lines (number on one line, «сум» / «so‘m» / «$» on the next).
- Text cut off, overlapping, hidden behind the bottom bar, the keyboard or the clock, or a button off-screen.
- A control smaller than about 44×44 pt (iOS) / 48×48 dp (Android) — estimate from the screenshot, say it is an estimate.
- Text in another language than the device language, or an English icon name read out.
- A section that is empty, shows an error, or looks like a phone layout stretched across a tablet.

## Report
AGENT_BRIEF.md format. report.md: one section per screen/section visited (name, screenshot ids, findings or «OK»). Then a list «Issues» sorted most severe first, each with screen, exact text and screenshot id. Mark anything you could not reach as NOT REACHED with the reason.
